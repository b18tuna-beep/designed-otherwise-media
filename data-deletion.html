Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Designed Otherwise does not create accounts for users or store personal data about viewers. Our tools only access our own channel accounts.

If you believe we hold any information about you, for example a reply to your comment, and want it removed:

  1. Send a message to the Designed Otherwise Facebook Page with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Tell us which post or comment it concerns.
  3. We will remove anything we control within 30 days and confirm by reply.

You can also delete your own comments at any time directly on the platform where you posted them.
