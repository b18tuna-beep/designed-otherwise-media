Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Designed Otherwise ("we") publishes educational videos on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. This policy explains what information our publishing tools handle.

What we collect

We do not sell products, run accounts for the public, or collect personal information from viewers through this website. This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Our internal publishing tools connect only to our own channel accounts through the official APIs of YouTube, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and our scheduling service. They read our own posts and aggregate performance numbers (views, likes, comment counts) so we can see how our videos perform. Public comments left on our posts may be read in order to reply to them.

How we use it

Only to publish, schedule and measure our own videos. We do not share, sell or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored encrypted. Aggregate metrics are kept for as long as the channel operates. Nothing is stored about individual viewers beyond what the platforms show publicly.

Your choices

To ask us to delete anything related to you, see the data deletion instructions.
